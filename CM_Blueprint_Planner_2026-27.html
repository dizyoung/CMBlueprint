This planner has a lot under the hood — Loops & Rhythm, per-book scheduling styles, daily load rules, and more. Everything stays available, but it's tucked behind "Advanced mode" (toggle in the top bar) so the basics stay simple while you're getting set up.

Children

Add each child you're planning for. Their Form determines which categories apply to them by default (you can refine per-category on the Categories tab). School days/week drives the pacing math.

School year

Set how many terms and weeks per term your year has. Exam weeks default to the last week of each term, or you can list specific weeks.

History cycle

Pick which cycle year your family is in this year (see History Cycles tab for details).

Forms

Forms are developmental stages, not strict grade levels. We collapse the traditional six PNEU Forms (I, II, III, IV, V, VI) into four bands for ease of planning — Form III/IV and Form V/VI are historically taught as combined programmes. Which Forms your family actually uses is determined by the children you add on the Our Family tab.

History cycle

4-year cycle. Spine options: Story of the World, Mystery of History, or Biblioplan. Set your current cycle on the Our Family tab.

Weeks behind schedule 0

Loops control the daily rhythm: each loop is an ordered list of slots that points at a subcategory's book queue, a specific book, a task, or a custom item. Daily View pulls only the CURRENT item from each scheduled loop — not every book in the loop. Checking an item off advances both the book/subcategory queue and the loop itself.
