CM Blueprint

A Charlotte Mason planning workbench — broad and flexible, not tied to any one curriculum or scheduled programme.

Beta preview: your plan saves in this browser as you go. Use Export Backup to protect your work or move it to another device. Today, Term Plan, Progress, accounts, and cloud sync are not built yet.

How it works

  1. 1 Build your plan → Subjects & Books Add work items for each subject. Set who they're for, choose your resources (or mark them as practice — no book needed), and decide what's active, optional, or not this year.
  2. 2 See the map at a glance → Family School Map A read-only grid of every active work item — who it's for across the top, subjects across the side. Good for spotting gaps and sharing with a spouse or co-op coordinator.
  3. 3 Shape your week → Weekly Rhythm Place your active work items into a weekly rhythm — morning time, group lessons, individual lessons. Broad blocks, not a minute-by-minute schedule.
  4. 4 Check resources → Book & Resource List See every book and material rolled up from your plan — what you have, what you still need to buy, and what you haven't chosen yet. Open the Family School Map and scroll to Book & Resource List.

Not built yet

Today's assignments, pacing math (sittings / buffer), term planning, progress tracking, accounts, and cloud sync are intentionally out of scope for this phase.
