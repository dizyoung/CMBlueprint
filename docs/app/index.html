CM Blueprint

A Charlotte Mason planning workbench — start with the wide room, then move into real life, then place the work, then choose the books.

Beta preview: your plan saves in this browser as you go. Use Export Backup to protect your work or move it to another device. Today, Term Plan, Progress, accounts, and cloud sync are not built yet.

How it works

  1. 1 See the wide plan → Family School Map Start here. The Resource Map shows who needs what — subjects, strands, resources, lesson length, and frequency. Click any item to edit it or add a resource. This is your CM feast laid out on a grid.
  2. 2 Edit the plan → Subjects & Books Add or edit individual work items, set form applicability, choose resources, add lesson length and frequency guidance, and mark what's active, optional, co-op, or not this year.
  3. 3 Place the work → Weekly Rhythm Drop subjects into a weekly rhythm — morning together time, individual blocks, loop slots, co-op afternoon. Broad blocks, not a minute-by-minute schedule.
  4. 4 Check the books → Book & Resource List Every book and material rolled up from your plan — what you have, what you need to buy, and what you haven't chosen yet. Open the Family School Map and scroll to Book & Resource List.

Not built yet

Real-life constraints layer (co-op days, blocked times, lighter days), Today's assignments, pacing math, term planning, progress tracking, accounts, and cloud sync are intentionally out of scope for this phase.
