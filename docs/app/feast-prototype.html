What this prototype touches

This page records three separate things per strand: whether you are using it this year, who it is for, and which loop (if any) holds it. Changing one never clears the others. It creates, edits and deletes nothing else — cards, loops, sequences, resources and your Weekly Rhythm are untouched, and everything else here is derived live on every render.
