Everyone, availability, and group availability are always derived — they are never stored twice.

Five steps. Nothing here creates teaching cards — it only records the decisions the rest of the app keeps asking for.

Feast Planning → build:setup-prototype

Testing guide

  1. Enter two or more children.
  2. Confirm their Forms.
  3. Create a custom group.
  4. Add children to it.
  5. Give one child a light-independent day and another a full day.
  6. Create one loop bucket and confirm the buckets look right.
  7. Continue to Feast Planning.
  8. Assign a strand to the custom group.
  9. Confirm the group members and consequence text are visible.
